Materials are ultra-detailed: the matte texture of the book covers, gold foil catching the light, crisp printed pages, smooth white plaster on the house model, reflective glass on the tablet screen, warm brass on the key. Photorealistic rendering, 16:9 aspect ratio, product catalog quality. Tack-sharp focus across every object. Minimal, elegant, Apple-meets-luxury-education styling. No text overlays, no branding clutter. Shot on Phase One IQ4 150MP, 120mm macro lens, f/8, studio strobe lighting with large softbox above and white bounce cards on sides. Ultra-sharp detail, 8K quality downsampled to 4K.

Prompt C — Video Transition
START FRAME: A curated stack of premium property investment books with a miniature luxury house model sitting on top, beside a tablet showing analytics and a brass key. Clean black background, soft studio lighting, editorial product photography. Everything still and pristine. END FRAME: A dramatic freeze-frame explosion of wealth — hundreds of £50 notes, gold bars, gold coins, property deeds stamped "SOLD", and brass keys erupting outward from the centre. The books are mid-shatter, pages transforming into banknotes. The house model is cracking open with golden light and coins spilling from inside. High-speed photography freeze on black background. TRANSITION: The scene begins still and quiet — the books, house model, tablet, and key sit perfectly arranged. Hold for 1 second. Then a subtle golden glow begins to emanate from inside the miniature house model, pulsing gently. The glow intensifies over 0.5 seconds — light leaking from the windows and cracks. BANG — the transformation erupts. The house model cracks open and gold coins burst from within. Simultaneously, the book pages begin ripping upward, each page morphing mid-air from printed text into the pattern of a £50 note. The book covers blow apart. Banknotes multiply and fan outward in every direction. Gold bars tumble into frame from above. Gold coins shower down like confetti. Property deeds with red "SOLD" stamps spiral outward. Brass keys fly from the centre. The explosion expands over 2 seconds in ultra-smooth slow motion — every note, every coin, every gold bar rendered in crisp detail as it flies. Individual page fibres visible as they tear. Banknote holograms shimmer as they rotate. Gold surfaces catch and reflect studio light. The motion peaks, then begins to slow further — the final 1 second is an extreme slow-motion hold as the last elements drift outward, creating the freeze-frame composition. Everything settles into the final exploded arrangement. Hold the final frame for 1 second. STYLE: Photorealistic, black background throughout, consistent studio lighting with added rim light on flying objects. No camera movement — locked-off tripod shot. The only motion is the transformation from education to wealth. Cinematic, aspirational, luxury advertising feel. Think Rolex commercial meets property investment brand. DURATION: 5-6 seconds total. ASPECT RATIO: 16:9 QUALITY: High fidelity, smooth 24fps minimum (60fps preferred for slow-motion detail), no artifacts. VARIATIONS: — Reverse version: Start with the wealth explosion frozen in space, then everything pulls inward and assembles back into the neat book stack and house model (powerful "it all starts with education" narrative) — Gold-focus version: Remove the banknotes entirely, make it purely gold bars, gold coins, and golden light — more premium, less "cash grab" feel — Looping version: Education → glow → explosion → slow reverse → education → repeat
Recommended Settings
Image generator: 16:9 aspect ratio, highest quality/resolution available
Video model: 16:9, 5-6 seconds, highest quality, 60fps if available for slow-motion
Tip: Generate the education shot first (Prompt A), then reference it when generating the explosion (Prompt B) so the book covers, house model, and key match exactly. For the video, upload Prompt A as start frame and Prompt B as end frame.
Narrative tip: The reverse version (wealth assembling back into education) often performs better on social — it tells the story "this is where it all begins" which resonates with aspiring investors.
